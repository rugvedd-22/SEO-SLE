What is SEO

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the process of improving a website to increase its visibility on search engines like Google and Bing. It helps a website appear higher in search results when users search for related information. SEO improves website traffic, makes content easier to discover, and helps users find relevant information easily.

Importance of SEO

SEO helps websites appear in search engine results. It increases website visibility and helps users find useful information.

How SEO Works

Search engines discover websites, crawl their pages, index their content and rank them according to relevance.
